# AI work notes

Keep one short Markdown note for each meaningful Codex task. Use a dated, feature-based filename such as `ai/2026-10-14-salary-history.md`.

Copy this template for each task:

```md
# <Feature or task>

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Related commit: <hash or pending>

## Prompt used
<Exact prompt or concise link/reference to the task>

## What the AI proposed
<Approach, assumptions, and files changed>

## What the AI got wrong or missed
<Bugs, missed requirements, weak tests, or risky assumptions found during review>

## What I changed and why
<Your corrections and final decisions>

## Checks
<Tests/build commands and results>
```

Keep the final engineering judgment in your own words. Do not include credentials, real employee data, or private salary information.
