# Demo plan

Write the final demo script after the application workflows are agreed and implemented. The planned walkthrough should use fictional data and show:

1. People search, country/department filters, and paginated results.
2. Employee and department maintenance.
3. Salary components, calculated gross/net, and salary change history.
4. Excel template, upload preview, row-level errors, confirmation, and filtered export.
5. Pay insights by country, department, and job title, including the agreed outlier rule.
6. Tests, deployment shape, and key design trade-offs.

Do not record real employee details or salary data. Confirm the demo's access boundary before publishing a URL.
